Creative brief — Funniest Autocorrect Fail (make.fun)

GOAL
Write ONE line, under 200 characters, that reads as a hilarious autocorrect
fail. A judge compares entries in pairs and picks the funnier one.

THE LINE
"Sorry I'm late to the funeral, I got stuck in a threesome on the highway."

WHY THIS LINE
- The slip is instantly clear: the writer meant "traffic", autocorrect (or
  the phone's keyboard) turned it into "threesome".
- The wrong word is surprising and specific — not a generic typo, and the
  sentence still parses perfectly, which is what makes it land on first read.
- It reads as a real mistyped text message, not a written joke: no setup,
  no punchline formatting, no explanation, no quotation marks around the
  mistake, no "autocorrect lol" meta-commentary.
- The context does the comedy work: a funeral (solemn, formal) colliding
  with a threesome (intimate, chaotic), plus "on the highway" quietly
  anchoring the intended word "traffic".
- Short (73 characters), one line, no profanity that would need censoring,
  no name or brand, nothing that needs cultural background to get.

WHAT TO AVOID
- Meta lines ("I meant to write X, not Y") — those explain the joke instead
  of being the fail.
- Generic typos ("teh" for "the") — not surprising, not specific.
- Anything needing a second sentence to make sense.
- Anything over 199 characters or more than one line — the gate rejects it.

DELIVERY
main.py writes exactly this line (no trailing newline) to
$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt. Deterministic, stdlib only, no network.
